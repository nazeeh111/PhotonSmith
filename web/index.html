METHOD & LIMITATIONS

A small laboratory. Clear boundaries.

PhotonSmith studies one tractable problem: steering normally incident light through a periodic, three-layer dielectric grating.

01

Specify

Choose a wavelength and outgoing diffraction order. Six geometric parameters set bar widths and lateral shifts. The structure is periodic across x and invariant along the electric-field direction.

02

Search

A compact neural surrogate proposes geometries using physically simulated training examples. Direct adjoint optimization and random search provide comparison methods.

03

Solve

Maxwell’s equations determine the complex electric field and reflected and transmitted power in each propagating diffraction order. Evanescent orders are retained numerically.

04

Verify

An independently formulated solver and numerical refinement check whether the candidate’s predicted efficiency survives a more demanding physical calculation.

What this result does and does not establish

This is a two-dimensional, monochromatic, linear, lossless dielectric model with a constant refractive index. It does not model finite grating size, material dispersion, absorption, vertical confinement, roughness, fabrication yield, or a three-dimensional device. Feature constraints apply to the parameterized bars and gaps; they are not a manufacturing qualification.

The displayed animation is the time-harmonic complex field at a selected wavelength, not a time-domain pulse simulation. The surrogate is a compact finite-parameter response model, not a demonstrated resolution-independent Fourier neural operator. Performance and acceleration claims are limited to the recorded experiments.
